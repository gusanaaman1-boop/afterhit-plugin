AFTERHIT 1.0 - by Gussa Naaman

The hit stays sharp and close; a space blooms after its attack and closes
before the next hit. Four controls: HIT, SPACE, TAIL, GATE.

What is installed, and where:

  VST3 plug-in    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\AFTERHIT.vst3
  Standalone      C:\Program Files\Naaman\AFTERHIT\AFTERHIT.exe
  Manual          C:\Program Files\Naaman\AFTERHIT\AFTERHIT-MANUAL.md

Close Cubase (or any DAW) before installing. After installing, Cubase finds
AFTERHIT under Effects > Reverb.

This installer is not code-signed. If Windows SmartScreen appears, choose
"More info" and then "Run anyway".

To remove it: Windows Settings > Apps > AFTERHIT > Uninstall.
